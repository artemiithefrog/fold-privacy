Set up fold counting

Fold is made for iPhone Duo. Automatic counting uses a Shortcuts automation and requires iOS 27.2 or later.

  1. In Shortcuts, tap +, then Edit. Find Automation → Display → Opened or Closed. Select Closed only.
  2. Add the action Fold → Record Fold.
  3. Choose Run Immediately if prompted, then save.
  4. Fold and unfold your phone, then open Fold. One new fold should appear.

You can also start from Set Up Counting in Fold, which opens Shortcuts for you.

Track time open

Make a second automation the same way: Display → Opened or Closed, select Opened only, and add Fold → Record Open with Run Immediately. Time open is measured from each opening to the next closing. It does not change your fold count.

Missing or extra folds

Use exactly one automation for Closed and one for Opened. Duplicate automations, or running Record Fold by hand, add extra entries. Folds from before setup cannot be recovered, and folds are not recorded while an automation is turned off or if iOS skips a run. After a restart, unlock your phone once so Fold can save new entries.

Add a widget

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Fold. Small shows your count; Medium adds a week of activity. Lock Screen widgets are available while customizing your Lock Screen. iOS schedules widget updates, so a new fold may take a little time to appear.

Delete your history

Your history is stored only on your device. See the Privacy Policy for removing all app data and managing backups.
