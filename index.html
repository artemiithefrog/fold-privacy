Privacy Policy

Fold counts how often you fold your iPhone Duo, shows how long it stays open, and includes a small hinge game.

Effective date: 11 November 2026
Developer: Artemii Malyshev

Your fold history stays on your device.

Fold has no account system, server, advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. Saving images to Photos, Apple's TestFlight services, optional support messages, and this website are described below.

1. Data used by the app

Fold stores your fold history locally on your device: the time of each fold recorded by its Shortcuts action, your daily fold totals, and a list of your most recent events. If you also set up the Opened automation, Fold stores when your phone was opened and closed and the time-open totals it calculates from those times. It also keeps your share card settings and your best Catch the Angle score. The app and its widgets share this local history so the widgets can show your counts.

While Fold is open, it reads the hinge position and approximate angle that iOS provides. It uses these readings to show the live hinge status and to run the Catch the Angle game. Live hinge readings are not saved; only your best game score is kept.

Fold does not upload your history to the developer and does not operate a server for your data. The app does not collect advertising identifiers or request access to your location, contacts, microphone, or camera.

2. Shortcuts automations

Fold's actions (Record Fold, Record Open, and Get Fold Count) run through Apple's Shortcuts app. You create the automations that run them, and you can turn them off or delete them in Shortcuts at any time. Fold cannot see or change your other shortcuts or automations.

Each run of Record Fold or Record Open adds a time-stamped entry to your local history. Get Fold Count only reads your saved total.

3. Photos and sharing

When you tap Save on a stats card, Fold asks for permission to add images to your photo library and saves only the image you created. It uses add-only access, so it cannot see, read, or change the photos already in your library.

When you share a stats card or a game score, the image goes to the app or person you choose through the iOS share sheet. Fold does not receive a copy of what you send; the service you share to handles it under its own privacy policy.

4. Storage, backups, and deletion

Your fold history remains on your device until you remove the app's data. To remove all local Fold data, use Delete App in iOS Settings or on the Home Screen. Offloading an app keeps its documents and data. Removing a widget or turning off an automation stops showing or recording folds but does not delete your history.

Device backups may include app data according to your Apple or computer backup settings. Fold does not provide its own cloud synchronization. The developer cannot remotely access or delete the history on your device.

5. Apple services and TestFlight

Apple may process installation, purchase, and diagnostic information when you use the App Store or Apple's services, under the Apple Privacy Policy. Apple may make diagnostic or aggregate app reports available to the developer according to the service and your settings.

If you use a beta through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares beta-testing information with the developer. This can include the app and OS version, device model, sessions, crashes, and feedback. Your name and email may be available for email invitations; accepting a public invitation link alone does not disclose them to the developer. Feedback can include information and screenshots you choose to submit.

We use TestFlight information to investigate bugs and improve Fold. We do not sell it or share it with third parties. See TestFlight & Privacy for Apple's processing and retention. To stop beta participation, use TestFlight's Stop Testing control. You may contact us about data available to us; Apple also provides its Data and Privacy portal for data it controls.

6. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use this information to reply, resolve your request, and improve the app when you report a problem. Please send only information relevant to your request.

Support email is delivered and stored using Apple iCloud Mail, under Apple's privacy policy. Support correspondence and any copies of beta feedback we retain are kept only as long as needed to resolve the request, investigate the issue, or meet applicable legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of information we hold.

7. This website

This website uses GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, cookies, external fonts, or tracking scripts to these pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security when serving GitHub Pages sites. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Your choices and requests

Where applicable law provides, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold, or object to or request restriction of its use. You can contact us using the email below. You may also contact your local data protection authority. For information stored only on your device, use the controls in section 4.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support or beta feedback, contact us so we can address it and delete information where appropriate.

9. Changes to this policy

If Fold's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date. Any required notice or permission will be provided before a new use of personal information begins.
